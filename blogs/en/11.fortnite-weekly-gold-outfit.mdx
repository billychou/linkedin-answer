## What is the Fortnite weekly gold outfit?

During the **Treasure Hunters** mini-season, Fortnite adds one Solid Gold outfit per week to the Treasure Chest loot pool in Battle Royale. Unlike Item Shop cosmetics, it costs nothing: there is no purchase, no Battle Pass tier, and no quest chain to grind. You simply open chests in any Battle Royale match and hope the week's gold outfit rolls out.

A few facts that shape the whole event:

- **One gold outfit per week.** Each weekly window features exactly one Solid Gold skin, plus a handful of themed extras (vehicle skins, Back Blings, Jam Tracks, Loading Screens).
- **The season is finite.** Treasure Hunters is scheduled to run through **December 5, 2026**, across five weekly rotations.
- **The drop is random.** There is no pity timer and no guarantee — every chest is an independent roll.

## How to get the weekly gold outfit for free

1. **Queue into any Battle Royale match** during the active week — solos, duos or squads all count.
2. **Land somewhere chest-dense** and start opening Treasure Chests; the weekly Solid Gold outfit is a random chest drop.
3. **Skip the shop and the quests.** The gold outfit requires no purchase, no Battle Pass tier and no quest chain — anything claiming otherwise is wrong.
4. **Keep farming chests across matches.** The drop rate is extremely low, so volume is your only lever.
5. **Claim it before the Sunday reset** (6AM PT / 9AM ET), when the rotation swaps in the next Solid Gold outfit and the current one leaves the chest pool.

## Do you need quests or a purchase? No

This is the most common misconception around the event. The season's **weekly quests award XP and season progression only** — they are completely separate from the gold outfit drop. Likewise, nothing about the weekly gold skin is purchasable: it never appears in the Item Shop, and no real-money or V-Buck transaction unlocks it. If a guide tells you to "complete the Treasure Hunters quests to unlock the gold skin," it is conflating two unrelated systems.

## Drop odds: how rare is the gold outfit?

Community tracking puts the weekly gold outfit at roughly **1 in 1,562,500 chests** — reportedly one of the rarest free drops Fortnite has ever shipped. Two practical consequences:

- **Every chest is another roll.** There is no trick, location bonus or streak that improves the odds per chest; chest-dense landings simply give you more rolls per match.
- **Treat it as a chase cosmetic.** Most players will never see it drop in a given week — and that is normal, not a bug. Unlike Item Shop exclusives, at least it costs nothing but play time.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is the Fortnite weekly gold outfit really free?

Yes. It is a random Treasure Chest drop in Battle Royale with no purchase, Battle Pass tier or quest requirement of any kind.

### Can I still get last week's gold outfit?

No. When the Sunday reset happens, the previous week's Solid Gold outfit leaves the chest pool and is no longer obtainable — which is why the archive matters for knowing what you missed.

### Does the gold outfit drop in other modes?

The drop is tied to Battle Royale Treasure Chests. Zero Build counts, since it shares the same chest loot; modes without standard chest loot are not a reliable place to farm it.
