# Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how LinkedIn Answer Today collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you visit our website or use our features. By using the site, you consent to the practices described below.

## Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us, such as:
- Account information if you sign in, including your name and email address
- Content you submit, such as messages sent through our AI chat feature
- Communication preferences and feedback you share with us

We also collect limited information automatically when you visit:
- Usage data, such as pages visited, time spent, and referring links
- Device and browser information, such as operating system and screen size
- Cookies and similar technologies, as described below

## How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:
- Provide and improve our services, including daily answers and mini games
- Maintain accounts, streaks, leaderboards, and invitations
- Respond to your questions and support requests
- Understand how visitors use the site and improve the user experience
- Keep the service secure and prevent abuse

## Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to remember preferences and understand usage. Our analytics providers, including Google Analytics, Plausible, and other services, may collect aggregated, anonymized usage statistics. We respect Do Not Track where supported and aim to keep tracking as privacy-friendly as possible.

## Advertising

Our site may display ads served by third-party advertising partners, such as Google AdSense. These partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising through Google's Ads Settings and other industry opt-out tools.

## Third-Party Services

We may share limited data with trusted third-party providers who help us operate the site, such as hosting, analytics, advertising, and authentication services. These providers are only permitted to use your information to perform services on our behalf and are required to protect it.

## Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information, and to object to or restrict certain processing. You can also choose not to create an account, clear your cookies, or use browser privacy features to limit tracking.

## Children's Privacy

Our service is intended for general audiences and is not directed to children under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will revise the "last updated" date above. Please review this page periodically to stay informed.

## Contact Information

For privacy-related questions or requests, please reach out through the contact details provided on this website.
